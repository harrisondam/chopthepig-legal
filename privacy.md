# Chop the Pig Privacy Policy

**DRAFT for review by a lawyer. This is not legal advice.**

Effective date: October 22, 2026
Version 1

Chop the Pig ("the game") is made and published by Harrison Dam, an individual developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, where it goes, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it. It covers the game on Steam, other PC stores, Android, and iOS.

The short version: **almost everything stays on your device.** The game has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and I do not run a server of my own. Information leaves your device only when you play online, and only so that online play, achievements, and leaderboards work. I never sell your information.

## 1. Information that stays on your device

The game saves these files on your device. I cannot see them unless you send them to me yourself.

- **Settings and progress:** your settings, runs in progress, unlocks, achievements, collection, tips seen, daily and challenge records.
- **Online records:** the players you have met, your table friends, your ladder against friends, your match history, and the players you have blocked. These hold other players' names and player ids.
- **Chat reports:** when you report a player, the game saves their name, their player id, and what they said in your reports file. It is not sent anywhere yet.
- **Feedback and crash reports:** when you use Send feedback, or after the game crashes, the game makes a folder with your note (if any), a screenshot, the table's log, the game's version, your operating system, your settings, and your saved run. These stay on your device until you choose to send them to me.
- **Run stats and the play-test log (optional):** if you turn on Run stats in Settings, the game keeps a record of your finished runs and every move in them, with times. Test builds that I send to friends keep this log too and pack it into a zip file next to the game, which you can send me if you want to.

On PC these files are in your user folder (on Windows: %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Chop the Pig). Deleting that folder deletes them. On phones, uninstalling the game deletes them.

## 2. Information that leaves your device when you play online

Before you first play online, the game shows a short notice and asks you to agree. Until you agree, the game does not sign in to any online service of its own. If you agree, the following happens.

**Epic Online Services (EOS).** The game uses Epic Games' online services so that players on every store and phone can play together. The game signs you in to EOS with an id that EOS makes for your device (with your device's operating system and model name) or, on Steam, with a ticket from Steam. EOS gives you a player id (a "Product User ID"). Through EOS the game sends and keeps:

- your display name: your Steam name when Steam is running, otherwise "Player";
- your achievements and the game statistics behind them;
- your online Classic results (wins, losses, and places) for the online leaderboard;
- your first daily run's score each day and up to three charms from it, for the daily leaderboard and other players' "ghost" tables;
- a copy of your last 50 online matches (the time, the places, and the players' names and points), so your match history follows you to other devices;
- the tables you host or join (the table's name, code, seats, and whether it is public).

Leaderboards are public: other players see your display name with your scores.

**Other players.** At an online table, the game sends the other players your display name, your player id, your card design, your moves, your chat messages, and your quick reactions. Chat is filtered for bad language and limited in speed. Chat messages are passed along while you play and are not saved by me, Epic, or Valve, except in another player's own reports file if they report you. Tables connect players directly, through Epic's or Valve's relay servers, which can see your IP address as part of running the connection.

**Steam (Valve).** On Steam, the game uses your Steam ID, Steam name, and Steam friends list to find your friends' tables, and sets your Steam "rich presence" so your Steam friends can see and join your table. Steam lobbies hold the same table details as EOS lobbies.

**Stores.** Steam, Google Play, and the App Store handle buying and downloading the game and may collect information under their own policies. The game itself does not see your payment details.

## 3. Why I use this information

- To run online tables, chat, friends, and invites (needed to provide the online features you ask for).
- To keep achievements, leaderboards, the daily board, and your match history (needed for those features).
- To keep the game safe: the chat filter, rate limits, mute, block, and report.
- To fix bugs and balance the game, but only from feedback, crash reports, and logs that you choose to send me.

Where the law needs a legal basis (for example the EU and UK GDPR): online features run on your consent (the online notice) and on what is needed to provide the service you asked for; safety features run on my legitimate interest in keeping tables safe; feedback you send runs on your consent.

## 4. Who receives it

- **Epic Games, Inc.** runs Epic Online Services. Epic's privacy policy: https://www.epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy
- **Valve Corporation** runs Steam. Valve's privacy policy: https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/
- **Google** runs Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Apple** runs the App Store: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- **Other players** at your tables, and anyone who views a leaderboard.
- **Me**, only for what you send me yourself (feedback, crash reports, play-test zips, emails).

I do not sell or rent your information, and I do not share it for advertising. I may disclose information if the law requires it.

These companies may store information in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, they use safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 5. How long it is kept

- Files on your device: until you delete them or uninstall the game. Feedback and crash folders keep only the newest 20.
- EOS achievements, statistics, leaderboard entries, and your match history file: while the game's online services run, or until you ask me to delete them.
- Lobbies: only while the table is open.
- Chat: not kept, except in another player's own reports file.
- Feedback, crash reports, and play-test zips you send me: until the issue is fixed or the test is over, and no longer than 12 months.

## 6. Children

You must be 16 or older to play online. The game is not made for children, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16, including children under 13. Before you first play online, the game asks you to confirm that you are 16 or older. If you are a parent and believe your child has used online play, contact me and I will delete their online information.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR, or California's CCPA and CPRA), you may have the right to:

- know what information I hold about you and get a copy of it;
- have it corrected or deleted;
- object to or limit how it is used;
- withdraw your consent at any time;
- not be treated differently for using these rights;
- complain to your local data protection authority.

How to use them:

- **On your device:** delete the game's folder or uninstall the game.
- **Online information:** contact me with your display name and, if you can, your player id (shown on the Friends screen as your friend code). I will delete your EOS records (achievements, statistics, leaderboard entries, and match history file), or ask Epic to where its tools need that, and answer within 30 days (45 days under the CCPA).
- **Withdraw consent:** in Settings, under Legal, open this Privacy Policy and choose Withdraw online consent, just under this line. The game stops signing in to Epic Online Services from the next start. This does not delete what was already sent; ask me for that.

## 8. Security

Online connections through Epic Online Services are encrypted. The game keeps nothing on a server of its own. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, but I keep what you send me private and delete it when it is no longer needed.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will update the effective date and the version. If a change is important, the game will show the online notice again before you next play online.
